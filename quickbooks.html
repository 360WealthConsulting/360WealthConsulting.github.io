Did you just disconnect 360 from QuickBooks? Then our access to your company has ended. We can no longer read anything from it. Our team will see the change and close the connection on our side, permanently destroying the stored access tokens. If you disconnected by mistake, or want us to delete the QuickBooks data we already hold, contact us (details below).

Why we connect

When 360 prepares your business’s taxes or reviews its finances, we need your books. Instead of asking you to export and e-mail reports, you can let us read them directly from QuickBooks Online, securely and with your permission. We see current figures, and you send fewer files.

What we read

  • Company information
  • Chart of accounts
  • Customers and vendors
  • Invoices and bills
  • Profit and Loss and Balance Sheet reports (accrual and cash basis)

What we never do

  • We never create, change or delete anything in your QuickBooks company.
  • We never initiate payments or move money.
  • We never store bank or card account numbers, tax identification numbers, your customers’ and vendors’ contact details, or notes.
  • We never access QuickBooks Payroll or Payments.
  • We never sell your data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

You can end our access at any time:

  • In QuickBooks Online: go to your company’s apps settings, find [DECISION: app name] among your connected apps, and choose Disconnect. Our access ends immediately.

Records we read before you disconnected stay in your client file and are kept as our retention rules require, unless you ask us to delete them and no legal obligation requires us to keep them.
