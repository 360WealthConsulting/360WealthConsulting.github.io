Why it matters

Why connected tax and business records matter.

  • Decisions have tax consequences

    Estimated payments, business structure and how you pay yourself can affect your taxes. It helps when the same team sees the business and household records together.

  • Fewer hand-offs

    Instead of carrying the same records between separate workflows, one relationship keeps the relevant tax and business information together.

  • Planning ahead, not catching up

    When your records and tax work stay connected, the conversation can happen before the deadline instead of after it.
