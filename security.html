Security

How your information is protected in 360Plus.

You trust us with your tax returns, statements and business records. These are the controls built into 360Plus, the technology our team uses to organize that information — described plainly.

Schedule a Consultation See the 360Plus Experience

Access

Who can see your information.

  • Staff sign-in with MFA

    Our team signs in through the firm's identity provider, and 360Plus requires multi-factor authentication by default.

  • Permissions by role

    Staff access is built from specific permissions, and requests without an explicit permission are denied rather than allowed.

  • Limited to the right people

    Staff see the client records they're assigned to, and sensitive sections of a record have their own permissions.

  • Invitation-only client access

    Clients join the 360Plus client portal only by invitation, and sign in with a one-time code sent to their invited email address; codes expire and attempts are limited.

Accountability

A record of what happened.

  • Append-only audit log

    Audit events cannot be updated or deleted — the database itself rejects it.

  • Tamper-evident entries

    Each audit entry is cryptographically chained to the one before it, so tampering is detectable.

  • Document access recorded

    Changes, denied requests and document access are written to the audit trail.

Protection in the software

Defenses built into 360Plus.

  • Browser security headers

    A strict Content Security Policy, framing denied and content-type sniffing disabled.

  • Signed, expiring sessions

    Session cookies are signed, HTTPS-only and expire after eight hours; sessions can be revoked.

  • Encrypted credentials

    Connection tokens, integration credentials and certain sensitive fields are encrypted at the field level.

This page describes security controls implemented in the 360Plus software. It is not a certification, audit report or contractual commitment.
