Your tax returns, financial records and business books are among the most sensitive information you have. This page describes, in plain terms, how we protect them in the 360Plus platform and our Client360 system. Our Privacy Policy explains what we collect and why.

Who can see your information

  • Only assigned staff. Each staff member has a role that defines what they can do, and most staff can see only the clients they are assigned to. Staff cannot give themselves more access.
  • Sensitive numbers are masked. Full Social Security numbers are never displayed in Client360; staff see at most the last four digits. Account numbers in portal summaries are shown by their last four digits.
  • Every access leaves a trail. Changes, document views and sign-ins are recorded in an audit log that cannot be edited or deleted, and that is chained so tampering would be detected.

How sign-in works

  • Staff sign in with firm-managed Microsoft accounts [DECISION: “with multi-factor authentication” — only after confirming OIDC_REQUIRE_MFA=true in production]. Staff sessions expire after 8 hours.
  • Clients are invited individually. You sign in with a one-time code sent to your e-mail address, which expires after 10 minutes, or with a passkey registered in person at our office. We store sign-in codes only in a hashed form, limit how many can be requested, and do not reveal whether an e-mail address has an account.
  • No passwords to steal. Client360 does not store client passwords at all.

Encryption

  • In transit: all connections to our website, Client360 and the portal use HTTPS.
  • Credentials: access tokens for QuickBooks, Microsoft and other connected services are encrypted before they are stored, and are never sent to a browser or written to logs.

What you can do

  • Protect your e-mail account with a strong password and multi-factor authentication; it receives your sign-in codes.
  • Never share a sign-in code. 360 staff will never ask you for one, or for your full Social Security number, by e-mail, text or phone.
  • Be wary of e-mails asking you to “re-verify” or open unexpected attachments. When in doubt, call us at a number you already know.
  • Sign out on shared devices, and tell us right away if a device with a passkey is lost.
