5. QuickBooks Online data

In short: our QuickBooks connection is read-only. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in QuickBooks and never moves money. We store only the fields we need, and we never store bank or card account numbers or tax identification numbers from QuickBooks.

How a business is connected

Only someone with administrator access to that business’s QuickBooks Online company can connect it. They sign in to Intuit, choose the company, and approve access on Intuit’s own consent screen. Intuit grants one authorization per company. We request only the QuickBooks Online Accounting permission (com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting), and not the Payroll or Payments permissions. We never see your Intuit password.

What we read

  • Company information
  • Chart of accounts
  • Customers and vendors
  • Invoices and bills
  • Profit and Loss and Balance Sheet reports, on both the accrual and cash basis

We read these when the business is first connected, and afterwards when our staff refresh them or our scheduled update runs [DECISION: state the schedule if the scheduled sync is turned on, e.g. every 6 hours]. After the first read, we fetch only what has changed.

What we store

For each record, a limited set of fields: QuickBooks identifiers and version numbers, names, types, dates, amounts, balances and links between records. For the two reports, we store the report lines, account references and totals exactly as QuickBooks reports them, as dated copies, so we can see how figures change over time.

What we never store from QuickBooks

  • bank or credit-card account numbers;
  • tax identification numbers (such as EINs or SSNs);
  • e-mail addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses of your customers and vendors;
  • free-text notes or memos.

How we use it, and who sees it

Only to provide the tax, accounting and advisory services your business engaged us for, such as preparing returns, estimating tax, reviewing financial position, and planning. Only 360 staff whose role allows it, and who are assigned to your business, can view or download it. The client portal shows only whether QuickBooks is connected and when it last updated; it does not display your accounting figures.

What we do not do with it

  • We do not sell it, rent it, or share it with data brokers or advertisers.
  • We do not use it to train artificial-intelligence models, and we do not send it to AI service providers.
  • We do not combine it with other businesses’ data or use it to benchmark or market to anyone.
  • We do not write anything back to QuickBooks.

How access is protected

The access tokens Intuit issues are encrypted before they are stored, are used only by our server, and are never displayed in a browser or written to logs. The sign-in hand-off with Intuit uses a single-use, randomly generated value that expires after 10 minutes and is tied to the staff member who started it.

Disconnecting

You can end our access at any time, in either of two ways:

  • From QuickBooks: in QuickBooks Online, open your company’s connected apps and disconnect [DECISION: app name as listed on Intuit, e.g. “Client360 by 360”]. Intuit stops our access immediately. Our system detects this the next time it tries to read, marks the connection as needing attention, and our staff then complete the disconnection on our side within [DECISION: e.g. 5 business days].
  • By asking us: contact us (see section 20). We disconnect it in Client360, which asks Intuit to revoke the authorization.

When we disconnect on our side, we permanently destroy the stored access tokens, and we read nothing further. The QuickBooks records and report copies already stored become part of your client file and are kept as described in section 11. If you want them deleted, tell us; we will delete them unless a law, regulation or professional obligation requires us to keep them, and we will tell you if that is the case. 10. How we protect information

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards designed to protect your information. They include:

  • Encryption in transit: connections to 360plus.ai, app.360plus.ai and the portal use HTTPS.
  • Encrypted credentials: access tokens for QuickBooks, Microsoft and other connected services are encrypted before storage and never shown in a browser.
  • Staff sign-in through the firm’s Microsoft accounts, with multi-factor authentication [DECISION: say this only after OIDC_REQUIRE_MFA=true in production — it is false today (checked 2026-10-31)].
