3. QuickBooks Online data

In short: our QuickBooks connection is read-only. It never creates, changes or deletes anything in QuickBooks and never moves money. We store only the fields we need, and we never store bank or card account numbers or tax identification numbers from QuickBooks.

How it is connected. A business is connected only when someone with administrator access to that business’s QuickBooks Online company signs in to Intuit and approves access on Intuit’s own consent screen. Intuit grants one authorization per company, using the QuickBooks Online Accounting permission (com.intuit.quickbooks.accounting). We do not request QuickBooks Payroll or Payments permissions.

What we read. Company information, the chart of accounts, customers, vendors, invoices and bills, and QuickBooks’ Profit and Loss and Balance Sheet reports (accrual and cash basis).

What we store. A limited set of fields from each record: identifiers, version numbers, names, types, dates, amounts, balances and links between records. For the two reports we store the report lines, account references and totals exactly as QuickBooks reports them, with the date of each copy.

What we never store from QuickBooks:

  • bank or credit-card account numbers;
  • tax identification numbers (such as EINs or SSNs);
  • e-mail addresses, phone numbers or postal addresses of your customers and vendors;
  • free-text notes.

How we use it. Only to provide the tax, accounting and advisory services you engaged us for: preparing returns, reviewing financial position, planning, and answering your questions. Authorized 360 staff can view and download the stored reports. The client portal shows only whether QuickBooks is connected and when it last updated.

What we do not do. We do not sell QuickBooks data, use it for advertising, share it with data brokers, or use it to train artificial-intelligence models.

Credentials. We never see your Intuit password. The access tokens Intuit issues are stored only in encrypted form, are used only by our server, and are never shown in a browser or written to logs.

Disconnecting. You can end our access at any time:

  • in QuickBooks Online, by disconnecting the 360 app from your company’s connected apps [DECISION: app name as listed on Intuit, e.g. “Client360”]; or
  • by asking us (see Contact us); we disconnect it from Client360.

When we disconnect, we ask Intuit to revoke the authorization and we permanently destroy the stored tokens. No further data is read after that. QuickBooks data already stored becomes part of your client file and is kept as described in section 8; you may ask us to delete it, subject to the record retention rules we must follow.

QuickBooks and Intuit are trademarks of Intuit Inc. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Intuit. Your use of QuickBooks is governed by Intuit’s own terms and privacy statement.
