Why we built 360Plus

Business owners often have information spread across returns, bookkeeping records, payroll, email and document folders. We built 360Plus to give our team one organized operating picture.

How it works

360Plus connects records, documents, tax work, tasks and communication around the right person, household or business. Client-facing portal capabilities are introduced in stages as they are enabled.
